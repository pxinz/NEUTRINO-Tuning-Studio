The icon is intentionally responsive:

- 16–48 px: compact “TS” mark
- 64 px: “TS for” mark
- 128–1024 px: full “TS for NEUTRINO” lockup

All sizes are rasterized from the supplied artwork with nearest-neighbor scaling.
